Quotations, step by step

Make a priced offer for an account, then open it, send it and revise it when the customer asks for changes. You end with a quotation that carries its own number, versions and PDF.

Create a quotationOpen a quotation and send itChange, revise or copy a quotation

Create a quotation

Use this to price work for an account and keep it as a draft.

1

Click + Create quote

Open Quotations from the Sales menu. The button is at the top right of the page.

2

Click Select → on Quotation

You see three offer types: Quotation, Technical Offer and Budgetary Offer. Choose Quotation for a full priced offer.

3

Choose the Account

Pick the customer in ACCOUNT. A tag such as Direct customer appears under it. The account is the only required field.

4

Choose the Contact

Open CONTACT and pick the person who receives the offer. The list shows contacts of the account you chose.

5

Type the Quote name

Write a short name for the job. Quote ID says "Assigned on save"; Date and Valid until are filled for you.

6

Write the Scope of work

Describe what you will do in the box under SOW 1. Use B, I and the list buttons to format it. + Add SOW adds another block.

7

Type the line description

In PARTICULARS, type what you are selling on the first line. From catalog under it picks an item from your products instead.

8

Pick the UOM

Choose the unit of measure, for example Nos, Job or Set. CATEGORY next to it is optional.

9

Enter QTY and RATE (₹)

Type the quantity and the rate. DISC % gives a discount on this line only. AMOUNT is calculated for you.

10

Set the Discount

In the SUMMARY panel choose % or ₹ and type the discount for the whole quote. The amount is taken off at once.

11

Enter the GST %

Type the tax rate. The summary adds a GST line and shows the Grand total.

12

Click Save as draft

This keeps the quotation as a draft with its number. Send to customer below it is marked "Coming soon" on this form; send from the saved quotation (see the next flow).

Open a quotation and send it

Use this when a quotation is ready to go to the customer.

1

Click List

On the Quotations page, use the view switcher. List shows one row per quotation with its number, age, value and status.

2

Click Open on a row

Point at a row. Open and Copy appear at the right end. Click Open.

3

Click the status chip

The chip next to All quotations shows the status. Click it to see the choices: Draft, Pending and PO Received. Pick one to change the status.

4

Click More

The menu has Download PDF, Download CSV, Adapt layout, Copy quote, Create standard quote, Email quote and WhatsApp.

5

Click Email quote

A dialog opens with the subject and message already written. The quote PDF is attached for you.

6

Type the address in TO

Enter the customer email. Edit SUBJECT and BODY if you want.

7

Click Send email

The email goes out with the PDF. Use Cancel to close the dialog without sending. WhatsApp in the same menu is the other way to send it.

Change, revise or copy a quotation

Use this when the customer asks for changes or you need a similar quote.

1

Click Edit quote

This opens the quotation in the same form you used to create it. Change lines, prices or text and save.

2

Click Versions · 2

Each revision is listed with its reference, status and date. Here Rev 1 is current and Rev 2 is QT-2026-0013-R2.

3

Click Create new version

Use this to answer a change request without losing what you sent before. The new version gets an -R reference.

4

Click Copy quote

In More, choose Copy quote to start a new quotation from this one, for example for another account.
