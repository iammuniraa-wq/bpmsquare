Your data

Your workspace is yours.

One workspace per company, on its own domain.
Data separated at the database level. Every row is tied to your workspace.
Personal data encrypted at rest. Phones, emails, tax ids.
Roles decide who sees what. Supervisors see their sites. Employees see themselves.
Change history on everything. Who, what, when, before and after.
Test workspaces cannot email real customers. Ever.
Custom fields and layouts without touching code.
Your customisations stay yours. They never change another company's product.
People and access in one place. An admin adds a person, sets a role, and every change is logged. A new login must choose its own password at first sign-in.
Secrets are shown once. Signing secrets and API keys cannot be read back after you copy them.
