Accounts, step by step

Add a company, find it again, read everything about it, and bring many in from a spreadsheet. Each step shows the exact button to click.

Add an accountFind an account and see everything about itImport many accounts

Add an account

Do this when a new company becomes a customer, a prospect or a partner.

1

Click Master data in the left menu

The globe icon opens the Accounts list. It shows how many accounts you have.

2

Click + New Account

The button is at the top right of the list. The form opens on its own page.

3

Type the Company name

Only Company name and Account type are required. Required fields carry a star.

4

Choose the Account type

The list has Prospect, Direct Customer, OEM / Dealer and End Customer.

5

Fill in the address and contact details

These are optional. Type the City, then use the Communication, Business and Notes & Referral sections if you have the details.

6

Click Save

Save is at the bottom of the form. Cancel leaves without saving.

Find an account and see everything about it

Do this when you need the history of one customer: contacts, cases, quotations, and how the relationship is going.

1

Type in Search accounts...

Type part of the name. The list stays as it is until you click Filter. The All types list next to it narrows by account type.

2

Click Filter

The list now shows only the matches. Clear appears next to the button and removes the search.

3

Click Open →

Open → is at the end of the row. The account page opens.

4

Read the top of the page

The name, the type and the account ID are at the top. Cards show City, Phone, Email and Customer since.

5

Click Contacts

The tabs are Overview, Cases, Contacts, Assets, Quotations, Projects, Invoices, Estimates and Activity. A number on a tab counts its records.

6

Click Account 360

It is at the top right of the page. A side panel opens with a summary of the whole relationship.

7

Read the health score

A letter and a score out of 100 sit at the top, then What to do next. Click How is this scored? to see the working behind the score.

8

Scroll down to the cards

There is one card for each area: Pipeline, Revenue, Service, People, Installed base, Contracts and Sales coverage. The small i next to a card title explains it. 1 item on a card opens its list.

9

Click the close button

The X at the top right of the panel closes it. You are back on the account page.

Import many accounts

Do this when you have a list of companies in Excel or CSV. This guide stops before anything is saved.

1

Click Data Workbench in the left menu

The clipboard icon opens Data Workbench. It has three tabs: Import, Export and Update. Stay on Import.

2

Click Accounts under What to import

The screen shows the steps Upload, Match columns, Review and Result. A line says Required: Name, Type. Everything else is optional.

3

Download the template if you want one

↓ Download Accounts template gives you a file with the right columns and example rows. Replace the examples with your data. This guide does not download it.

4

Drop your file in the upload box

Drag a file onto the box or click it to browse. It takes Excel (.xlsx) or CSV up to 15 MB. Your own column names are fine.

5

Read the summary

Match columns opens with three counts: columns matched, columns ignored, and required fields not yet matched. Here it says 4 columns matched, 2 ignored and 1 required field unmapped.

6

Choose a field for each column

In the IMPORTS INTO column, pick the account field for each column of your file. Here Company name is set to Name (required). A column set to — don't import — is left out.

7

Click Continue to review →

The button works once every required field is matched. The next step, Review, shows what will happen before anything is saved. This guide stops here.
