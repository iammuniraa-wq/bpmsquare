Built for the GCC

Where labour is supplied by the man-hour, the hour has to be provable.

Manpower supply, facility management, MEP and oil-and-gas services in the UAE and Saudi Arabia bill clients per man-hour or man-day, and man-hour disputes are the normal state of affairs. BPMSquare puts a face, a location and a time behind every line on the invoice.

Overtime at 125 and 150 percent, six-hour Ramadan days, Friday–Saturday weekends, WPS and Mudad payroll rails, and the e-invoicing deadlines in both countries: see what is live today and what is scheduled.
