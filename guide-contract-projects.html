Contract projects, step by step

A won tender becomes a contract project. You allocate the budget, buy out packages from suppliers, record client changes and billing, and hand the budget to the ERP.

Open a project and read its numbersAllocate the budgetBuy out packagesClient changes, billing and handover

Open a project and read its numbers

The project shown is SAMPLE-RSC-2026-041.

1

Click Open on a project

The Contract Projects list shows contract value, budget and margin. The filters are All, Draft, Baseline waiting, Active and Closed.

2

Click the tender link

The link at the top left goes back to the tender the project came from.

3

Read the project path

Draft, Baseline waiting, Active, Handed over, Closed. A tick marks a step reached.

4

Read Key figures

Contract value, Budget and Planned margin. The section collapses.

5

Click Fix

Needs you lists what a person must fix, each with a link. Here: 26 lines without a cost code.

6

Read Where the budget goes

One bar of the contract value: the budget by cost type and the rest, your margin.

Allocate the budget

You decide the budget. The system does not make it for you.

1

Click Allocate by

Choose BOQ line to type a cost on each line, or Work package to type one cost per package. Open the Budget tab first.

2

Read the three figures

Contract value, Allocated and Margin so far. Margin is the contract value less what you allocated, live.

3

Click Allocated cost

Type a cost for each line. Left is the client's amount less your cost. Ordered shows what buy-out has ordered. Save allocation is at the bottom.

4

Click Revision

The list holds Baseline · Approved and later revisions such as Revision 1 · Draft.

5

Click Work breakdown (WBS)

It groups BOQ lines into work packages. It shows how many packages exist and how many lines are unassigned.

6

Click New work package

The panel asks for Code, Name, Owner, the Lines to include and a Note. Save it with Save work package. This guide did not save one.

Buy out packages

Group budget lines into packages, ask suppliers for quotes and award them.

1

Click Buy-out

The tab lists the packages and the supplier group they are bought from.

2

Read the three tiles

Packages, Ordered vs budget and Needs decision.

3

Read the packages

One row per package: Budget, Best quote, Ordered, Against budget, Status and ePROMIS. Chips above filter by status.

4

Click + New package

Enter a Name, choose the Kind (Supplier or Subcontract) and tick the budget lines. Create package saves it. This guide did not create one.

5

Click a package

The panel shows its budget lines, the quotes in the current round with Award package, the rounds kept for audit and the figures against budget.

6

Click Send a new round

Tick the suppliers, set Reply by and send the request. The form was opened but not sent.

Client changes, billing and handover

Record what the client changes after the award, bill in milestones and hand the budget to the ERP.

1

Click Client changes

The tab holds change orders from the client.

2

Read the three tiles

Approved variations, Revised contract value and Waiting for approval. An approved change moves the contract value.

3

Click + Add variation

The panel asks for a Title, the Change in contract value, the Change in cost, a Change request and What changed. This guide did not save one.

4

Click Client billing

The tab shows Contracted, Invoiced and Still to invoice.

5

Click + Add milestone

A milestone has a Title, a Due date and an amount as a percent of contract value or a fixed amount. This guide did not save one.

6

Read the billing schedule

Each milestone shows its amount before tax, its status and its invoice. Create invoice raises the invoice for a planned milestone.

7

Click Handover

The tab holds Hand over to the ERP.

8

Click Download Excel

It downloads the budget file for the current revision. Send to ePROMIS sits next to it, grey here.

Bring a tender. Leave with a priced bid.

Twenty minutes on a live workspace. Your BOQ, your suppliers.
