Estimates, step by step

Pick a template, enter the sizes and the margin, and read the price and the bill of materials. Use an estimate to price rows of a tender.

Build an estimate and read the priceUse an estimate on a tender

Build an estimate and read the price

The example is the Floor tiles (illustrative) template.

1

Click + New Estimate

The button is on the Estimates list.

2

Click a template

"Choose a template. You can paste the customer's email on the next page." Here: Floor tiles (illustrative). Others include Chain-link fence, Pipes and fittings, Porta cabin and Precast elements.

3

Click Source: start from an email

The section is closed by default. Open, it asks you to paste the customer's email, or you can fill in the details below.

4

Click Room length (m)

Under 2. About the floor enter the room, the tile and the layout. Each field shows its allowed range.

5

Click Margin on cost (%)

It is added to the cost to give the price. The customer only sees the price. Change any number and the model and the price update at once.

6

Read 3. Price

The total including VAT, then Tiles and materials, Laying and finishing, Delivery and site, Overhead, Total before VAT and VAT 18%. Tile range options compares Basic, Standard and Premium.

7

Click How the price is built

The section is for your team. Open, it shows the cost build-up as a bar and a list.

8

Click Bill of materials

The section is for your team and the factory. Open, it lists each item with unit, quantity, unit cost and cost, and an Edit link.

Use an estimate on a tender

An estimate can price rows of a tender BOQ. The Tender management guide shows the whole tender.

1

Click Costing

On the tender, the Costing tab lists estimates and RFQs as Cost sources.

2

Click Add rows

It gives an estimate more BOQ rows to price.

3

Click Apply to 3 rows

Applies the estimate's cost to the ticked rows, with the markup. This guide did not apply it.

4

Click Open

It opens the estimate.

5

Click Back to the BOQ

The estimate shows its tender at the top. Back to the BOQ returns you to the rows it prices.
