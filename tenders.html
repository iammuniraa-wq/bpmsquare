Bid decision and bill of quantities

Bid with your eyes open.

Every tender starts with a recorded decision, and the bill of quantities arrives as the client sent it.

  • Start a tender from an incoming email: the shared inbox turns it into a tender deal, and you choose which senders and subject words are taken in
  • A bid or no-bid proposal that an approver signs off, with the reason kept
  • Upload the client's Excel; a revised upload keeps the work you have done
  • Match each row to your product catalogue with ranked suggestions

Costing

Price every row from a source you can name.

Rows are priced from an estimate, a rate or a supplier's quote. The screen counts what is priced, what still needs a cost, which RFQs are out and which replies are in.

  • Work a cost out from an estimate template: choose the kind of work, enter the inputs, and the price and bill of materials follow
  • Send RFQs to suppliers by email, each with a no-login reply link and an Excel reply sheet
  • Compare replies side by side and award the lowest valid price
  • Prices older than your validity window are flagged before they reach a bid
app.bpmsquare.com

Compare and award: each supplier's reply side by side, the lowest valid price marked.

The bid

Submit a version. Change it. Compare.

Each submission is a numbered version that never changes afterwards. Revise the bid and the next one becomes the next version. Submitting saves the version in BPMSquare; you send the priced Excel to the client yourself.

  • Compare any two versions line by line
  • The priced bill of quantities goes back in the client's own layout
Lines without a price are counted and listed.
Prices older than your window are flagged.
No approved decision is called out.
Bid guarantee not yet submitted is called out.
After the award

The win becomes a project, with a budget.

When the tender is awarded, create the contract project from it. The budget starts from what you priced, and buy-out follows the same RFQ and award routine.

  • Budget by resource and cost type, with revisions and a baseline you approve
  • Buy-out packages that send RFQs, compare replies and commit the award against the budget
  • Variations, a billing schedule and invoicing from the same project
  • An Excel hand-over of the budget and of each awarded package, for your purchasing system
  • Planned margin, committed cost and what still needs a decision, at a glance
