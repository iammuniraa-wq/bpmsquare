People and roles, step by step

Settings, People is where you add logins and decide what each person can reach. Only an administrator sees it.

Invite a person

Do this to give a colleague a login to your workspace.

1

Click + Invite

Open Settings, then People under USERS & ACCESS. The People tab lists everyone with a login, with their employee record, role and status. Click + Invite at the top right.

2

Type the Email

Type the email address of the person, for example colleague@company.com.

3

Choose a Role

Choose Member or Admin. The “i” icon next to Role explains the difference.

4

Leave or set a Password

Password is optional. The “i” icon explains what happens when you leave it empty.

5

Choose an Employee

Link the login to an employee record from the list, or leave None. The “i” icon explains why you would link one.

6

Click Send invite

Send invite creates the login. Close at the top of the panel leaves without saving.

See what a role allows

Do this to check which screens a business role reaches before you give it to someone.

1

Click the Roles tab

Click Roles next to People. The number on the tab is how many roles there are.

2

Find New role

The table lists each role with how many areas it opens and how many people hold it. + New role at the top right makes your own.

3

Click a role

Click a row, for example Sales User. The ready-made roles carry a Standard tag.

4

Read the Access list

A panel lists every area of the product. A ticked area is open to the role. For areas that hold records, Create, Edit and Delete show what the role may do there. The “i” icon explains the list.

5

Click Duplicate and edit

A standard role cannot be changed. Duplicate and edit makes a copy you can change.
