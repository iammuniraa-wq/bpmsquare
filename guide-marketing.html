Marketing campaigns and segments, step by step

Build a target group

Do this to save an audience, such as all prospects in one city, so you can reuse it in campaigns.

1

Click + New target group

Open Segmentation from the sidebar. The list shows each saved group with its conditions. Click + New target group at the top right.

2

Type a name

Click Target group name and type a name, for example Precast buyers in Mumbai.

3

Click Account type

Under Attributes, click an attribute to add it as a condition. You can also drag it onto the canvas. Attributes are grouped as Account info, Location, Business, Engagement and more.

4

Choose a value

The condition appears under Conditions. Choose is or is not, then a value such as Prospect.

5

Click City

Click a second attribute to add another condition. It is joined to the first with AND. Type the city, for example Mumbai, in the box next to it.

6

Choose Match ALL or Match ANY

With two or more conditions, choose whether an account must meet all of them or any one of them.

7

Read the recipient count

The line under the form shows → N recipients for the rule. Accounts with no email address are counted separately. In this sample workspace no account is in Mumbai, so it shows 0.

8

Click Create target group

This saves the group. It then shows in the Segmentation list and in the campaign form under Load a saved target group…. Below the rule you can also add or remove single accounts, or add email addresses that are not in the system.

Write a campaign

Do this to email a message to an audience. A campaign is saved as a draft until you send it.

1

Click + New Campaign

Open Marketing campaigns from the sidebar. The list shows name, subject, status and how many emails were sent, failed or skipped. Click + New Campaign.

2

Type a name

Type a name in Campaign name (internal). Only your team sees it. The campaign is saved as a draft as soon as you type.

3

Pick a template

Click START FROM A TEMPLATE to open the occasion tiles, for example Diwali, Holi, Eid, Christmas and Service Reminder. Each tile has designs to start from.

4

Write the Subject

Type the subject line. You can put the account name in with {{company_name}}. The “i” icon next to the label explains the fields you can use.

5

Write the message

Type the text in the MESSAGE box. The toolbar has B for bold, I for italic, a bullet list and a numbered list.

6

Choose the Target audience

Tick one or more account types: Direct customer, End-customer (under OEM), OEM / Vendor or Prospect. The number in brackets is how many accounts have that type.

7

Or load a saved target group

Instead of ticking types, pick a group you built before from Load a saved target group….

8

Check the recipient count

→ N recipients updates as you choose. Under the types you can also search for single accounts to add or remove, and add email addresses that are not in the system.

9

Click Save changes

This keeps the draft so you can come back to it. Nothing is sent.

10

Click Send now

Click Send now only when the text and the audience are right. To see the result afterwards, follow “Check how a campaign did”.

Start a newsletter

Do this to build a designed email with blocks, a preview and an unsubscribe link.

1

Click + New Newsletter

Open Newsletters under Marketing. The list shows ID, name, category, languages, status and last update. The Templates tab holds your reusable designs. Click + New Newsletter.

2

Type a name

In the Start panel, type a name in Name, for example October update.

3

Choose a Category

Choose Newsletter, Offer, Event, Announcement or Festival.

4

Choose a Consent topic

Choose the topic this mail is about: Offers, New products, Service reminders or Events, or leave Any consent topic. The “i” icon explains how topics are used.

5

Choose how to start

Write it for me drafts the mail from one line you give. Start from a design opens your templates and the built-in designs. Blank gives only a header and footer.

6

Check the Preview

Switch between Desktop and Phone, and between Light and Dark, to see how the mail looks.

7

Find the Unsubscribe link

Every newsletter ends with your address and two links, Unsubscribe and Manage preferences. You do not add them yourself.

8

Click Send...

Send... opens the send options. Save keeps a draft. Show me how starts a guided tour of this screen.

Check how a campaign did

Do this after a campaign went out, to see who got it and which leads came back.

1

Click Open →

In the Marketing campaigns list, click Open → on a sent campaign.

2

Read the four numbers

SENT, FAILED, SKIPPED and LEADS sit at the top of the campaign.

3

Look at the Recipients

The Recipients list shows each account that was mailed, with its status and the time it was sent.

4

Click Ready for sales

Open Leads under Marketing. The chips filter the list by stage. Click Ready for sales to see leads that sales should pick up.

5

Find the leads from a campaign

A lead that came from a campaign is named Interested: followed by the campaign name.

Set who you may email

Do this once, and again when you start mailing in a new country. These rules apply to every campaign and newsletter.

1

Click Consent

Open Settings. Under MARKETING, click Consent.

2

Choose Based in

Choose the country you send from. The “i” icon next to the label explains why it matters.

3

Read Rules by country

For each country the table shows who you may email Without a recorded yes: Only people who said yes, or Also existing customers.

4

Read the Topics

Topics are what people can say yes to: Offers, New products, Service reminders and Events. Each row says what it covers.

5

Click the Limits tab

The Limits tab sets when and how often messages may go out.

6

Set the quiet hours

Quiet from and Quiet until are the hours when no message goes out. Below them are Emails a day, per person, WhatsApp messages a week, per person and No journey emails on for the days you choose.

7

Click Save

Click Save at the top right after any change.
