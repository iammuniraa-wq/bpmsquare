Marketing, Email and Intelligence, step by step

Marketing reaches people. Intelligence shows who is ready. Build an audience, send an email, see who got it and who was left out and why, then find the leads that are ready. Consent rules decide who may be emailed at all.

Start from the Overview

Do this to see where you are. Marketing and Intelligence each open on an Overview with a short path of numbered steps.

1

Open the Marketing Overview

Click Marketing in the sidebar, then Overview. Four numbered steps show what to do next: Audience (who), Message (what), Review and send and What happened. Each has one number and one button.

2

Read the Always on row

Under the steps, Always on links to Journeys, Forms, Events and WhatsApp, each with a small count. Only the modules your workspace has are shown.

3

Open the Intelligence Overview

Click Intelligence in the sidebar, then Overview. It has up to four steps: Who is in market, Who is ready, Where to focus and What worked. A step shows only for a module your workspace has and you may open.

4

Click Scoring rules

This link under the steps opens the settings where a lead earns points. Scoring rules are not a sidebar row.

Build an audience

Do this to save a group of customers, such as all prospects in one city, so you can pick it again for emails, newsletters and programs.

1

Click + New audience

Open Audiences under Marketing. The list shows each saved audience with its conditions. Click + New audience at the top right.

2

Type a name

Click Audience name and type a name, for example Precast buyers in Mumbai.

3

Click Account type

Under Attributes, click an attribute to add it as a condition. You can also drag it onto the canvas. Attributes are grouped as Account info, Location, Business, Engagement and more.

4

Choose a value

The condition appears under Conditions. Choose is or is not, then a value such as Prospect.

5

Click City

Click a second attribute to add another condition. It is joined to the first with AND. Type the city, for example Mumbai, in the box next to it.

6

Choose Match ALL or Match ANY

With two or more conditions, choose whether an account must meet all of them or any one of them.

7

Read the recipient count

The line under the form shows → N recipients for the rule. Accounts with no email address are counted separately. This is a quick count of accounts. The real count for an email, which also checks each person's consent, is on the email itself.

8

Click Create audience

This saves the audience. It then shows in the Audiences list and in the Audience list of a new email. Above the button you can also add or remove single accounts, or add an email address.

Send a plain email

Do this to email a message to an audience. The screen follows the order Audience, Message, Review and send. A draft is saved until you send it.

1

Click + New email

Open Email under Marketing. The Sends chip lists every email with its type, status, how many were sent, failed or left out. Click + New email.

2

Type a name

Type a name in Campaign name (internal). Only your team sees it.

3

Pick the Audience

Choose Everyone we can email, or one of your saved audiences. More opens account types, single accounts and extra addresses. Change anything and the list shows One-off (changed here), with Save as audience beside it.

4

Read Who will receive it

This is the real count, for example 8 will receive and 105 left out. It runs the same checks as the send: an email address, a contact, and consent for the topic. Under it is the count for each reason.

5

Follow a fix link

Each person who is left out has a plain reason and a link that fixes it: Open the account, Create contact or Open the contact. The first five are shown. Click Show all for the rest.

6

Add an outside address

Open More, type an address under Add an email address and click Add. A chip says Ready, Not a contact or No consent. Only a contact who agreed to the topic is emailed. For a stranger, click Create contact first.

7

Check the Consent chip

The chip shows the topic (Any topic) and a Change in Settings link. Topics and rules are set once in Settings, not on this screen.

8

Pick a template

Click START FROM A TEMPLATE to open the occasion tiles, for example Diwali, Holi, Eid, Christmas and Service Reminder. Each tile has designs to start from. You can skip this and write your own.

9

Write the Subject

Type the subject line. You can put the account name in with {{company_name}}. The “i” icon next to the label explains the fields you can use.

10

Write the message

Type the text in the MESSAGE box. The toolbar has B for bold, I for italic, a bullet list and a numbered list.

11

Click Save draft

This keeps the draft and opens it. Nothing is sent. Click Save draft again later and the same draft is updated, never copied.

12

Click Review and send

On the saved draft, click Review and send. It opens a panel. It does not send anything yet.

13

Read the review panel

The panel shows who will receive the email and who is left out and why, with the same fix links. If nobody can be emailed yet, the send button stays off and the panel says so.

14

Click Send to N

Click Send to N only when the text and the audience are right. The email goes out at once and cannot be recalled. Click Close instead to go back.

Check how an email did

Do this after an email went out, to see who got it, who was left out, and to finish a send that was cut short.

1

Click Open →

In the Email list, click Open → on a sent email. Newsletters you sent from the Studio are in the same list, with the type Newsletter.

2

Read the four numbers

SENT, FAILED, LEFT OUT and LEADS sit at the top. While a send is running the page counts by itself, for example Sending… 4 of 8. You do not need to reload.

3

Look at who was left out

The Recipients list names everyone. A person who was left out has a reason, such as Not a contact or No consent, and a link that fixes it.

4

Click Send the rest

If the email service reached its daily limit, some emails fail and the page says so. Click Send the rest when the limit has reset. It sends only to people whose email failed or was never tried. Nobody already sent is emailed again.

Start a newsletter

Do this to build a designed email with blocks, a preview and an unsubscribe link. Newsletters need Newsletter Studio.

1

Click Newsletters

Open Email under Marketing. With Newsletter Studio, two more chips appear next to Sends: Newsletters for your designs and Templates for reusable ones. Click Newsletters.

2

Click + New Newsletter

The list shows ID, name, category, languages, status and last update. Click + New Newsletter.

3

Type a name

In the Start panel, type a name in Name, for example October update.

4

Choose a Category

Choose Newsletter, Offer, Event, Announcement or Festival.

5

Choose a Consent topic

Choose the topic this mail is about: Offers, New products, Service reminders or Events, or leave Any consent topic. The “i” icon explains how topics are used.

6

Choose how to start

Write it for me drafts the mail from one line you give. Start from a design opens your templates and the built-in designs. Blank gives only a header and footer.

7

Check the Preview

Switch between Desktop and Phone, and between Light and Dark, to see how the mail looks.

8

Find the Unsubscribe link

Every newsletter ends with your address and two links, Unsubscribe and Manage preferences. You do not add them yourself.

9

Click Send...

Send... opens the send options: the audience, and the count of who will receive it and who is left out. Save keeps a draft. Show me how starts a guided tour of this screen.

Find who is ready

Do this to see which leads a salesperson should pick up. In a workspace with lead scoring, one Leads list has two views of the same leads.

1

Click Scored

Open Leads under Intelligence. A switch above the list reads All leads and Scored. All leads shows status and source. Scored adds the score, the stage, the owner and the time left to respond. Click Scored.

2

Click Ready for sales

The chips filter the list by stage. Click Ready for sales to see leads that sales should pick up. Overdue shows the ones past their deadline.

Set who you may email

Do this once, and again when you start mailing in a new country. These rules apply to every email and newsletter. The page opens in any workspace that has Marketing. On an email, the Change in Settings link next to the Consent chip opens it too.

1

Click Consent

Open Settings. Under MARKETING, click Consent.

2

Choose Based in

Choose the country you send from. The “i” icon next to the label explains why it matters.

3

Read Rules by country

For each country the table shows who you may email Without a recorded yes: Only people who said yes, or Also existing customers.

4

Read the Topics

Topics are what people can say yes to: Offers, New products, Service reminders and Events. Each row says what it covers.

5

Click the Limits tab

The Limits tab sets when and how often messages may go out.

6

Set the quiet hours

Quiet from and Quiet until are the hours when no message goes out. Below them are Emails a day, per person, WhatsApp messages a week, per person and No journey emails on for the days you choose.

7

Click Save

Click Save at the top right after any change.

See it on your own data.

Twenty minutes on a live workspace, with your files and your team.
