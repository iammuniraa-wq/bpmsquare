Why the Gulf

Three things make this region different, and all three favour verified hours.

◉

Labour is supplied, then billed

Secondment, outsourcing and site services are priced per worker per hour or per day. The client signs off on hours before paying. A verified punch is the strongest evidence there is.

◷

Overtime is regulated and priced

UAE: 125% for normal overtime, 150% between 22:00 and 04:00, and six-hour days in Ramadan. Saudi Arabia: basic plus 50%. Contractors must get it right on the cost side and pass it through on the billing side.

▦

Compliance creates buying moments

Saudi e-invoicing Phase 2 reached small businesses in 2026. The UAE mandate pilots from July 2026, with large businesses live from January 2027 and everyone else from July 2027. Payroll runs on WPS, Mudad, Qiwa and GOSI.

What exists today stops short. HR platforms (Bayzat, ZenHR, Jisr, PalmHR) do attendance and payroll and stop there. Timesheet tools (Harvest, TimeCamp, Zoho, Odoo) go from hours to invoice but the hours are typed in after the fact. ERPs cost jobs from approved timesheets and need an integrator. BPMSquare is the record in between: verified attendance, automatic attribution, and the invoice from the same record.
