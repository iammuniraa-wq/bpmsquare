Work orders, step by step

A work order is the job a technician does for a case. Raise it from the case, follow it by status, complete it and bill it.

Raise a work order from a caseFind a work orderComplete a work order and raise the invoice

Raise a work order from a case

When a case has an AMC contract or a quotation and the repair can be scheduled.

1

Click Create work order

On the case page, in the Work orders card on the right. If the card says to link a quotation or an AMC contract first, do that before raising the job.

2

Choose the Scheduled date

A panel called Create work order opens on the right. It starts with today's date.

3

Choose the Technician

The case's technician is picked for you. Change it, or choose Not assigned.

4

Click Create work order

The button is at the bottom of the panel. Close, top right of the panel, leaves without creating anything.

Find a work order

When you need one job, or all the jobs in one status.

1

Click a status tab

Scheduled, In Progress, Completed, Invoiced and All. The number in brackets is the count.

2

Search the list

Type a reference, account or asset, then click Filter.

3

Click Open →

Each row shows the reference, the case it came from, the account, the asset, the type (AMC or Billable), the technician, the date and the status.

Complete a work order and raise the invoice

When the technician has finished the job.

1

Click Mark Complete

On a work order that is In Progress, the button is top right. The job then appears under the Completed tab of the list.

2

Click Raise Invoice

On a Completed work order the same place shows Raise Invoice. The invoice is then found on the Invoices page.
