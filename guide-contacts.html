Contacts, step by step

Add a person, link them to an account, and find them again. Each step shows the exact button to click.

Add a contact and link it to an account

Do this when you meet a new person at a customer. Every contact belongs to one account.

1

Click + New Contact

Open the Contacts list first. An account's Contacts tab has its own + New Contact too.

2

Type the Full name

Full name and Account are required.

3

Choose the Account

This links the person to a company. The list holds all your accounts.

4

Type the Role / designation

Optional. Department, Birthday and LinkedIn URL are optional too.

5

Type the Primary phone

The Phone numbers section has Primary phone, Secondary phone and Third phone.

6

Type the Primary email

Email & web also has Secondary email and Website.

7

Click Save

Save is at the bottom. Cancel leaves without saving.

Find a contact and open it

Do this when you need a phone number, an email or the cases of one person.

1

Type in Search by name, role or account...

Type part of a name. The list stays as it is until you click Filter.

2

Click Filter

The list shows only the matches. Clear removes the search.

3

Click Open →

Open → is at the end of the row. The contact page opens.

4

Read the top of the page

The name, the contact ID and the role are at the top. Cards show Account, Phone, Email and City.

5

Click the account name

The blue account name opens the account the person belongs to.

6

Click Cases

The tabs are Overview, Marketing, Cases and Activity. The number on Cases counts this person's cases.
