Purchase orders and suppliers, step by step

Keep your suppliers in one list, order stock from them with a purchase order, and record what arrives so the inventory updates.

Add a supplierSee supplier groupsOrder stock from a supplierReceive stock against an order

Add a supplier

When you start buying from a new vendor or subcontractor.

1

Click + New Supplier

On the Suppliers page, top right.

2

Type the Name

Required.

3

Choose the Type

Vendor sells parts and materials. Subcontractor provides labour or specialist work. The third choice is both.

4

Type the City

Phone, Email and GSTIN are optional. Notes can hold payment terms and lead times.

5

Click Save

The supplier gets an ID such as SUP-0001.

See supplier groups

When you keep an approved list for a client.

1

Click Groups

On the Suppliers page. The tabs are All, Vendors, Subcontractors, Both and Groups.

2

Click + New group

Each group has a name, a client and its members. The list shows how many members each group has.

Order stock from a supplier

When you need parts from a vendor.

1

Click + New Purchase Order

On the Purchase Orders page, top right.

2

Choose the Supplier

Required. Order date is today's date. Linked quote and Linked case are optional.

3

Set the Expected delivery date

When the order should arrive. Notes and Terms are below it.

4

Click + Add line

A first line is already there. Use the button for each further item.

5

Pick the item From inventory

The description, unit, quantity and rate fill in from the inventory item. Change the Qty as needed. Choose — Free-text line — for something that is not in inventory.

6

Click Save

The order total is shown above the buttons.

Receive stock against an order

When the goods arrive.

1

Click Receive stock

On the page of an order that is Sent or Partially received. It opens the Lines tab.

2

Type the quantity received

One box per line, with the number still remaining. Type what actually arrived.

3

Add a Note

Optional, for example a delivery reference.

4

Click Record receipt

The Received column of the Line items table updates and the stock goes onto the inventory item.
