Inspection reports, step by step

The inspection report is written on the case, at the Inspection stage. Record the findings, the recommendations and a cost estimate, add photos, then send it to the customer.

Write and send an inspection report

Write and send an inspection report

When the technician has looked at the equipment.

1

Open the case at the Inspection stage

On the case page, the stage bar shows Inspection as the current stage. The Inspection report card is below the bar.

2

Click Edit

On a report that is already saved as Draft saved, Edit opens the form. A new report opens the form straight away.

3

Write the Findings

What the technician found. This box is required.

4

Write the Recommendations

The repair actions you recommend. This box is required.

5

Enter the Estimated cost

The field is named Estimated cost (₹, optional). Leave it empty if you do not have a figure.

6

Add the Inspection photos

Click Add more photos (Add photos on a new report). On a phone, choose Camera to shoot or Photos to pick from the gallery. Each photo can be up to 10 MB.

7

Click Save draft

Keeps the report as Draft saved. The customer sees nothing yet.

8

Click Send report to customer →

Under the button, a note says it marks the report as sent and that no email is delivered yet.

9

Or click Skip report — Create Quotation

Use it when you do not need a report and want to go straight to a quotation.

The report has no checklist: it is free text for findings and recommendations, one cost figure and photos. For the rest of the case, see Cases.
