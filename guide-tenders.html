Tender management, step by step

Take a tender from the client email to a won contract project. You price the bill of quantities, send the bid by email, keep every bid version, and hand the project to the ERP.

Start a tender

Do this when a tender arrives by email, or when you log one by hand.

1

Click the email

Open the Inbox and click the email from the client. Here it is Plot plan for fencing (DWG).

2

Click Create from this email

The button offers what to create from the email.

3

Click Deal

The menu lists Quote, Project, Case, Estimate, Lead and Deal. Deal is a tender deal with its bid end date and bid decision.

4

Or click + New tender

To start without an email, open Pipeline, go to the Tenders view and click + New tender.

5

Click Account

Choose the issuer's account. It is required, marked with a star.

6

Click Tender number

Type the number the client gave the tender. Issuer, Portal, Bid end date and Source are on the same form.

7

Click Save

The tender is created at the first step, Received. This guide did not save a new tender.

8

Read the path across the top

Seven steps: Received, Decision, BOQ, Costing, Bid, Submitted, Won or lost. A tick marks a finished step. The numbered dot is where you are.

9

Click Review bid decision

The blue Next row says what to do now and gives one main button. Here a go decision is waiting for an approver. When nobody has proposed one yet, the button reads Propose bid decision.

10

Read the decision panel and click Approve

The panel shows the proposal, a Comment box and the buttons Approve and Reject. A rejection needs a comment. The panel was closed without deciding.

11

Click Details

Details is a closed row with the main facts. It opens on a click. Its "i" explains it.

12

Read Bid decision

Open, the row shows Stage, Bid decision, Bid end date, Bid guarantee and Open work.

Price the BOQ

Load the client's bill of quantities, set a markup and price the lines. The tender shown from here is SAMPLE-NUD-2026-077.

1

Click Bill of quantities

The tab holds the client's lines.

2

Click Upload revised BOQ

Use it to load the client's Excel file (.xlsx). The columns are read for you. You check them, then create the lines. The upload screen was not opened in this guide.

3

Click Markup on cost

Type a percent. It is added to the cost to make the rate. Here it is 15.

4

Click Price all lines

It prices each line from supplier quotes first, then the bill of materials, then past projects. Only fresh prices are used. The button is grey here because every line is already priced.

5

Read the Priced tile

It shows how much of the BOQ is priced. Here it reads 100%, "all lines priced". The filters Unpriced and Stale list lines that still need work. Total is at cost plus markup.

6

Click the "i" next to Cost from

It explains where the cost behind a rate came from: an estimate, an awarded RFQ, a past project, the client's file or a hand-typed figure. Every column head has an "i".

7

Click an item number, 2.1

The row opens in a panel on the right. It shows Quantity, Rate and Where the cost came from.

8

Click Cost / unit

Type your own cost per unit. Save cost next to it stays grey until the cost changes.

9

Click Save cost

It saves your figure as Manual and keeps the source it replaced. This guide did not save a cost.

Cost rows from estimates and RFQs

When a row has no cost, price it from an estimate you build or from supplier quotes. The tender shown here is Floor tiles for a 120 m2 showroom.

1

Click Costing

The tab shows how each BOQ row gets its cost.

2

Read the four tiles

Rows priced, Still need a cost, RFQs out and Replies in. Each has an "i". Show them in the Bill of quantities jumps to the rows that still need a cost.

3

Read Cost sources

One table of estimates and RFQs. Prices BOQ rows names the rows each one prices. Each BOQ row takes its cost from one source.

4

Click New RFQ

It opens a new RFQ to ask suppliers for prices. New estimate sits next to it. The guides RFQs and supplier replies and Estimates show both step by step.

5

Click Add rows

It gives an estimate more BOQ rows to price. A panel lists the rows it can price, each with a tick, and a Markup %.

6

Click Apply to 3 rows

The button at the bottom applies the estimate's cost to the ticked rows. This guide did not apply it.

7

Click Open

It opens the estimate that prices the rows.

8

Click Back to the BOQ

The estimate shows its tender at the top (Back to the tender). Back to the BOQ returns you to the rows it prices. A tender estimate has no Create quote.

Submit and send the bid

Check the bid, read its versions and send the priced BOQ to the client by email. The tender shown is SAMPLE-NUD-2026-077, already at version 3.

1

Click Bid

The number on the tab is how many bids were submitted.

2

Read the tiles

Lines total now, Last submitted, Bids submitted and Vs previous bid. The green bar above them reads Ready to submit. If something is missing, it lists what.

3

Click Revise bid

Before the first submit this place holds Submit bid. It saves the lines and total as a numbered version and moves the tender to Submitted. Cost and markup are never saved in a version. This tender is already submitted, so the button reads Revise bid.

4

Click Compare

Under Compare versions choose From and To, then click Compare. Each version in the list above shows its total, who submitted it and why it was revised.

5

Choose Payment terms

The list holds the payment-term templates set up in Settings. This workspace has none yet, so it reads None chosen.

6

Click Send by email

It sits next to Download priced BOQ. A second one sits next to Export answer sheet. Costs and markup are never in the file.

7

Click To

A side panel opens. Type the client's address in To. Note is optional.

8

Click Send

The mail goes out through the tender. This guide did not send it.

9

Read Sent to client

It lists every file that went to the client. Here it reads "Nothing sent yet".

10

Click + Record a send

Use it for a portal upload or a mail sent outside the tender. The panel asks for Document, How, To, Version and Note.

Work with the client and the team

Keep client mail on the tender and talk to your team in the room, which the client never sees.

1

Click Interactions

Sent mail, replies and the source email form one timeline here.

2

Click New email

It writes to the client from the tender. This tender has no mail yet.

3

Click To

In the panel type the address, then write the Subject and Message. This guide did not send it.

4

Click Room

The team room is internal. It has four sub-tabs: Discussion, Team, Decisions and Documents. Log a call sits above them.

5

Click Team

It lists who is in the room. Add by name... adds someone.

6

Click Decisions

Pin one-line decisions, for example "Agreed to...".

7

Click Documents

It lists files attached to messages in Discussion.

Track competitors

Keep a short list of who else is bidding and what they may charge. The tender shown is SAMPLE-NUD-2026-077.

1

Click Competitors

The tab shows how many are on the tender and how many are expected below you.

2

Click Add competitor

To add one yourself. The list has three columns: Company, Expected price and Status.

3

Fill in the Add competitor panel

The panel asks for Company (required), Website, Country, Expected price, Strengths and a Note. Save it with Save. This guide did not save one.

4

Click a row, Amiantit

A panel opens with the evidence for that competitor. The status reads Added by you or Proposed.

5

Click Suggest from market research

It proposes competitors with a source for each fact. It uses part of your AI allowance, so use it once per tender.

6

Click Supply only

It first asks what it needs, starting with: is this a supply-only tender, or supply and install? Nothing runs until every question is answered. This guide did not run a search.

Win and hand over

Record the outcome, open the contract project and hand its budget to the ERP.

1

Click Record the outcome

The Next row of a submitted tender holds the button.

2

Click Won

The panel reads "Close this deal as..." with Won, Lost and Dropped. Confirm saves your choice.

3

Click Create contract project now

A won tender starts its contract project from here. This guide did not create one. Later the Next row offers Open contract project.

4

Click Open on a project

The Contract Projects list shows each project with its contract value, budget and margin.

5

Click the tender link

The link at the top left, here Tender OPP-0014, goes back to the tender the project came from.

6

Read the project path

Draft, Baseline waiting, Active, Handed over, Closed. The Contract projects guide covers each tab.

7

Click Handover

The tab holds Hand over to the ERP.

8

Click Download Excel

It downloads the budget file for the current revision. Send to ePROMIS sits next to it, grey here.
