Leads, step by step

Capture an enquiry, find it by status, and turn it into a deal. Each step shows the exact button to click.

Add a leadFind a lead and turn it into a deal

Add a lead

Do this when an enquiry arrives and you want to keep track of it before it becomes a deal.

1

Click + New Lead

The button is at the top right of the Leads list. A small window opens on top of the list.

2

Start typing in Account

Type part of the company name. Matching accounts appear under the field.

3

Click the account in the list

The name moves into the field. Use the small × to pick another one.

4

Type the Title

Say what the enquiry is about in a few words.

5

Choose the Source

The list has Direct, OEM Referral and AMC.

6

Click Save

Cancel closes the window without saving.

Find a lead and turn it into a deal

Do this when a lead is ready to be worked as a sales deal.

1

Click a status

The buttons at the top are New, Inspecting, Quoted, Won and Lost, each with a count. The list shows only that status.

2

Type in Search lead title or account...

Use it with a status or alone.

3

Click Filter

The list updates to the matches.

4

Check the Status

The status shows as a tag on the row. This list has no control to change it.

5

Click → Deal

One click creates a deal from the lead and opens the deal page. The button reads Converting… while it works. If it fails, a short message appears under the button. This guide does not click it.
